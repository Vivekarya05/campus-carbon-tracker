Synthetic demo data. Kingsford Technical University is fictional and all campus activity data (meter readings, bills, trips, waste) is generated. Emission factors come from the Australian National Greenhouse Accounts Factors and UK DESNZ conversion factors; grid intensity comes from AEMO data. Every number is reproducible with python -m pipeline.run.
